## 🎥 RECORDING TIPS

### **Software to Use:**
- **Loom** (easiest - automatic upload)
- **OBS Studio** (free, professional)
- **Zoom** (record yourself talking)
- **Windows Game Bar** (Win + G)

### **Best Practices:**
1. **Clear your browser cache first** (Ctrl + Shift + Delete)
2. **Close unnecessary tabs** - only demo tab open
3. **Zoom browser to 100%** for clarity
4. **Speak slowly and clearly** - simple English
5. **Show your face** (optional but recommended)
6. **Test audio** before recording
7. **Record in quiet environment**
8. **Practice once** before final recording

### **Screen Setup:**
- Full screen browser
- Hide bookmarks bar
- Close developer tools
- No distracting notifications
